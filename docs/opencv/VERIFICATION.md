## Limits of this verification

- No consented human exercise dataset, trainer form-rating agreement or subgroup evaluation was performed.
- Fake-camera and no-person VP8 checks validate model loading, actual inference and abstention/input lifecycle; they do not establish pose accuracy on people.
- The app uses explicit exercise selection and heuristic posture suggestions. A trained automatic exercise recognizer was not integrated, and no external classifier accuracy is claimed.
- The controller and planning tests do not establish safety, medical efficacy, personalized nutrition adequacy or measured muscle activation.
- Docker container execution and actual AWS deployment/workload/latency/cost were not tested. The Docker and AWS CLIs are absent. CloudFormation linting verifies the template locally, not resource creation.
- COOL/Graviton is not implemented. A new judge-accessible fitness video, code access arrangement and live endpoint/screen-share are pending.
- Chromium is the verified browser target. Other browser engines, physical camera devices and mobile hardware require separate validation.
